# Rewards Policy — BigCity Cashback

Document: rewards_policy.txt
Audience: SUPPORT, CAMPAIGN_OPERATIONS
Purpose: Base cashback rules for eligible purchases

## Tier cashback rates

- Gold customers receive 10% cashback on eligible purchases.
- Silver customers receive 5% cashback on eligible purchases.
- Bronze customers receive 2% cashback on eligible purchases.

## Caps and credit timing

- The maximum cashback per transaction is ₹500.
- Base cashback is credited within 48 hours after successful transaction settlement.
- Only settled, non-refunded, non-cancelled transactions are eligible.

## Eligibility exclusions

- Transactions that are refunded or cancelled are not eligible for cashback rewards.
- Pending or unsettled transactions do not receive cashback until settlement completes.
- Purchases that fall outside active campaign rules do not receive campaign bonus cashback (base tier cashback may still apply if otherwise eligible).

## Common reasons cashback was not credited

Use this section when a customer asks why they did not receive cashback or "money back":

1. The transaction is not yet settled (cashback waits until settlement).
2. The transaction was refunded or cancelled (not eligible).
3. The cashback amount would exceed the ₹500 per-transaction maximum (amount is capped).
4. The customer tier rate does not apply to that purchase category (not an eligible purchase).
5. Campaign bonus conditions were not met (see campaigns.txt).
6. Referral reward conditions were not met (referred user must complete an eligible transaction).

## Policy note for investigators

Support should confirm transaction settlement status and refund status before promising a credit.
Do not invent cashback amounts. Use tier %, campaign rules, and the ₹500 cap from this policy.
