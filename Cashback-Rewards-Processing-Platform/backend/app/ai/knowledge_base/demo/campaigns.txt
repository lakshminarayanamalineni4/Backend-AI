# Campaigns — BigCity Promotions

Document: campaigns.txt
Audience: CAMPAIGN_OPERATIONS, SUPPORT
Purpose: Active campaign rules and eligibility

## Festival Bonanza Campaign

- Customers earn an additional 5% cashback during the Festival Bonanza campaign period.
- Festival Bonanza bonus is in addition to base tier cashback when the purchase is eligible.
- Maximum Festival Bonanza campaign cashback is ₹1000 per customer per month.
- Eligible transactions must be settled successfully.
- Refunded or cancelled transactions are NOT eligible for Festival Bonanza cashback.
- Festival Bonanza bonus cashback is credited within 72 hours after transaction settlement.

Example eligibility check:
- Question: Is a refunded transaction eligible for Festival Bonanza cashback?
- Answer: No. Refunded or cancelled transactions are not eligible for Festival Bonanza cashback.

## New User Welcome Campaign

- New customers receive ₹200 welcome rewards after their first successful purchase.
- The first purchase must settle successfully.
- If the first purchase is refunded or cancelled, the welcome reward is not credited.

## Referral Campaign

- Customers receive ₹100 for each successful referral.
- The referred user must complete at least one eligible transaction.
- Eligible means settled, not refunded, and not cancelled.
- Referral reward is not paid if the referred user’s transaction fails eligibility checks.

## Campaign vs base policy

- Base tier cashback rules live in rewards_policy.txt (including the ₹500 per-transaction maximum for base cashback).
- Campaign bonuses may have their own monthly caps and credit windows (for example Festival Bonanza: ₹1000/month, 72-hour credit).
- When base policy and campaign timing differ, report both and apply the rule that matches the reward type (base vs campaign bonus).
