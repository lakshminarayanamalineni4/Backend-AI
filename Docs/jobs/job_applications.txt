# Daily Job Search Plan (Monday–Friday)

## Priority 1: Referrals (Highest ROI)

* Send 5 referral requests daily
* Target employees from product companies and startups
* Attach resume when possible

Goal: 25 referrals/week

---

## Priority 2: Direct Career Site Applications

* Apply to 5 matching jobs daily
* Focus on:

  * Python Backend Developer
  * Backend Engineer
  * Software Engineer
  * AI Backend Engineer
  * Cloud Backend Engineer

Goal: 25 quality applications/week

---

## Priority 3: LinkedIn Easy Apply

* Apply to 10 jobs daily
* Prioritize jobs posted within the last 24–72 hours

Goal: 50 applications/week

---

## Priority 4: Instahyre & Wellfound

* Apply to 5 jobs daily

Goal: 25 applications/week

---

## Priority 5: Auto-Apply Tools (Simplify, etc.)

* Use for 10–20 additional applications daily
* Only for jobs matching your experience level (1–3 years)

Goal: 50–100 applications/week

---

## Daily Preparation

* 1 hour Backend (FastAPI, Django, PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ)
* 30 mins AWS (S3, EC2, IAM, Lambda, CloudWatch)
* 30 mins DSA

---

## Daily Numbers

* 5 Referral Requests
* 5 Direct Applications
* 10 LinkedIn Applications
* 5 Instahyre/Wellfound Applications
* 10–20 Auto Applications

Total: 35–45 applications/day

Target: 150–200 applications/month + 100 referral requests/month
