TRANSFORMER:

    A Transformer is a neural network architecture that uses Self-Attention to understand relationship between words and process entire sequences(like words and sentences) in parallel.

    - It is the core technology behind modern LLMs such as ChatGPT, GPT, and Llama, enabling them to understand context, generate text, translate languages, summarize documents, and perform many other AI tasks.


Self Attention:

    a mechanism where each token looks at other tokens in the same sequence to decide which ones are important for understanding its meaning.

    Consider:

    - The animal didn't cross the street because it was tired.

        To understand "it", the model should focus on:

        animal

    Self-attention learns these relationships by assigning higher attention scores to relevant words.
